CASE 01

Classic Cosmetics · Tradeflow POS

Retail · POS · multi-store inventory
Active engagement
Challenge

A multi-location cosmetics retailer needed a POS with real controls, stock they could trust across locations, and a purchasing flow that matched how goods actually arrive.

Scope I led

Specification of a custom cosmetics app with the development team, POS design and bug triage on ERPNext v16, purchase invoice workflow, stock and ledger reconciliation, print formats, WhatsApp notifications, cashier training.

Decisions that mattered
  • Workflow instead of Purchase Receipt for invoice confirmation, modelled in Desk-style mockups before any build.
  • Locked-down roles. Separate cashier and salesperson architecture, with shift lifecycle and permissions enforced through the Role Permission Manager.
  • Verified, not assumed. Stock and ledgers reconciled across three locations with fuzzy matching; a permissions fault traced to owner and warehouse mismatches.
  • Counts that stick. A 7-day rolling count of 1,947 items split across 7 counters, tracked item by item.
Outcome

A POS with defined roles and shift controls, stock verified across locations, and cashiers assessed against a competency matrix.

CASE 02

REEVON

Athletic apparel · E-commerce · Shopify + ERPNext
Handed over
Challenge

A new apparel brand selling on Shopify needed orders, invoicing and dispatch connected in one system, with local payments and correct tax from day one.

Scope I led

Shopify integration, payment gateway selection, tax templates, branded documents, dispatch notifications, email configuration and handover.

Decisions that mattered
  • Gateway chosen on evidence. Compared four providers on readiness and KYC timing; IntaSend went live while others stayed in reserve.
  • Order chain mapped end to end from Sales Order to Sales Invoice to Delivery Note.
  • Customers told automatically. A Server Script sends the dispatch notification when a Delivery Note is submitted.
  • Documents that print reliably. Branded formats built with PDF-safe layouts, and a letterhead fix for a domain mismatch.
Outcome

Orders flow from Shopify through to dispatch notification, with Kenya VAT and import duty templates in place. The project reached handover.

CASE 03

Greentech Landscaping

Landscaping · Services · Mauritius · MUR
Active engagement
Challenge

A landscaping company running on QuickBooks needed installation projects and recurring maintenance visits managed in one system.

Scope I led

Requirements document, gap analysis of the custom app build, accounting migration from QuickBooks, module fit assessment and branded documents.

Decisions that mattered
  • Use the standard where it fits. ERPNext Projects recommended for installation jobs.
  • Build only where it doesn't. A custom recurring-visit build for maintenance, rather than bending a module out of shape.
  • Clean migration. The chart of accounts was brought across from a QuickBooks export, 283 rows in MUR.
  • Safe styling. Six print formats with namespaced CSS so they never clash with Frappe's own styles.
Outcome

Requirements and gap analysis completed, accounts migrated, six branded documents delivered, and a clear boundary between standard and custom.

CASE 04

Ilala Crest Lodge

Hospitality · Malawi · MWK
Active engagement
Challenge

A lodge needed an accounting foundation that mirrored its real books, so opening balances would land correctly on day one.

Scope I led

Chart of accounts design, import preparation, discovery questionnaire and document branding.

Decisions that mattered
  • Start from the real trial balance, not a generic template: 118 accounts that balance to the client's own figures.
  • Import-ready from the start. Reformatted to the ERPNext v16 importer specification and merged with 45 additional accounts.
  • Questions before configuration. A discovery questionnaire captured operations before design began.
Outcome

A balanced, import-ready chart of accounts built from the client's actual books, plus branded delivery documents.

CASE 05

Planet Active

Equipment leasing & manufacturing · Tanzania · TZS
Active engagement
Challenge

A pool table leasing business needed to track what goes into each table and where every table is, by serial number.

Scope I led

Requirements review, BOM and serial tracking design, compliance review and item master clean-up.

Decisions that mattered
  • Compliance raised early. A Tanzania VAT and EFD gap was flagged before build, when it is cheap to address.
  • Design around serial numbers, linking bills of materials to individual tables.
  • Item master fixed first. A 127-item raw materials price list in TZS was audited and its code scheme conflicts resolved.
Outcome

Validated requirements, a compliance risk surfaced up front, and a clean item foundation to build on.
