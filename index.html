How I work

One accountable lead, from the first workshop to life after go-live.

Five stages, one chain. Each ends with something tangible you can review, sign off and keep.

  1. 01

    Requirements

    I learn the operation before touching the system, so nothing is built on assumptions.

    You receive
    • Discovery questionnaire
    • Functional requirements document
    • Gap analysis
  2. 02

    Design

    Workflows, permissions and accounts are designed and shown before they are built.

    You receive
    • Workflow mockups
    • Role and permission matrix
    • Chart of accounts
  3. 03

    Build & test

    Configuration and data migration, checked against your real figures.

    You receive
    • Configured, tested system
    • Reconciled opening data
    • Branded print formats
  4. 04

    Training & go-live

    Each role is trained and assessed, and I lead the cutover so launch day is controlled.

    You receive
    • Role-based training
    • Competency assessment
    • Led cutover plan
  5. 05

    Post go-live care

    Real usage always surfaces refinements. I stay accountable for them.

    You receive
    • Issue resolution
    • Workflow and permission tuning
    • A named point of contact
